## Overview

This project coordinates public visual assets across surviving Symaira products without merging their product behavior. Product repositories remain canonical for shipped assets; project-local OpenDesign kits are reviewed handoff copies.

## Color roles

Warm black is the structural canvas; champagne and sand carry the family identity. Product accents identify a product but never replace text or status semantics.

## Typography

Use Roboto Flex Variable where distributed, with system fallbacks. Use monospace only for commands, paths, versions, and structured output.

## Layout and composition

Use one focal mark, generous safe space, thumbnail-readable product names, and restrained atmospheric grids. Keep product name and command separate from the mark.

## Asset surfaces

Maintain GitHub 1280×640, Open Graph 1200×630, square 1080×1080, release/README 1600×900, editable SVG, and adjacent PNG exports.

## App icons

Native app icons remain owned by each product repository. Use product-owned foreground glyphs and the shared Symaira icon rules. Do not invent an Apple app icon for CLI-only Browse or Fritz.

## Accessibility and motion

Keep live text where possible, pass text contrast, preserve visible focus, and avoid motion in static release assets. UI fixtures honor reduced motion.

## Product boundaries

Do not revive deleted products, invent a generic hub, or imply hosted, billing, cloud, or GUI capabilities absent from the current repository.

## Provenance

Every copied or generated source must be declared in the product `assets/provenance.json`; no secret or personal content may enter an asset.
